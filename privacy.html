Pit Captain

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-31

Pit Captain is an AI race-engineer companion for Gran Turismo 7. This policy explains what the app accesses, what leaves your device, and your choices. Plain language, no surprises.

What the app accesses

  • Your local network. The app reads Gran Turismo 7 telemetry that your PlayStation broadcasts on your home Wi-Fi (UDP port 33740). Your PlayStation's IP address is used only to receive that stream and is stored on your device.
  • Race telemetry. While a session is running, the app forwards the raw GT7 telemetry stream to the Pit Captain cloud so the engineer can analyze your driving and speak callouts back to you in near real time.
  • Your Discord ID. Used as your account identifier so your profile, lap times, garage, and tournament membership are tied to you. You provide it (or sign in with Discord); the app does not read your Discord messages or contacts.
  • Profile you enter. Driver name, engineer voice, skill level, units, which callouts you want, and the parts you mark as owned per car.

What we do with it

  • Telemetry is processed in the cloud to generate coaching audio and to record your lap times and mapping progress. Lap times, profile, garage, and cluster membership are stored so the app works across sessions.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

Microphone & voice

  • Only on the map, during a race. While you're viewing the track map during an active race, the app listens for the wake phrase "Hey Captain" so you can ask questions hands-free. It is never always-on and is never used in the background — leaving the map, muting the mic, or ending the race stops it. A mute button on the map turns it off.
  • On-device transcription. Your speech is converted to text on your device using Apple's speech recognition. Only the text of your question is sent to answer it — your voice audio never leaves your phone and is not stored.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not access your camera, photos, contacts, or location. The microphone is used only when you tap to ask a question (see Microphone & voice above).
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not send your PlayStation's IP address anywhere outside your own network; it stays on your device.

Third parties

  • Cloud hosting (Fly.io) processes telemetry and stores your profile/times.
  • Discord is used for sign-in and community membership, subject to Discord's own privacy policy.
  • Text-to-speech is used to generate the engineer's voice from callout text.

Pit Captain is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Sony Interactive Entertainment, Polyphony Digital, or Gran Turismo.

Data retention & deletion

Your cloud data is kept while your account is active. To request export or deletion of your data, contact us at the address below and include your Discord ID.

Children

Pit Captain is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
